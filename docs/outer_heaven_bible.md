# Outer Heaven — Story Bible
*Heaven's Mandate narrative skin v3 (replaces SIGNAL/108). Water Margin x Psycho-Pass in an FF7-style city.*

## Premise

One reactor-lit megacity where machines, magic, monsters and spirits openly coexist — plates above, undercity below. The city is governed by **the Commission**, a godlike AI that assigns every citizen an identity and a job at birth and arrests crimes before they happen. It rules in the name of peace. There has never been peace.

People who break from their assigned path — or get flagged for crimes they haven't committed — are labeled **Deviants**. The hunted ones wash up at **Outer Heaven**, a drowned amusement park on the under-lake, where an ex-clerk called **Timely Rain** feeds every stray the city spits out.

**The truth (Act 4):** the Commission is not an AI. It is a council of human brains, centuries old, living in jars under the tower — deviant minds who lived too long, running the world on their own bias. And Outer Heaven is a farm: injustices are manufactured to herd the most exceptional Deviants into one place, monsters and wars filter them, the amnesty is the final selection round, and the survivors' brains are "ascended" into the council. The 108-star legend ("108 stars fall from heaven, live as heroes, and are called home when they die") is Commission propaganda so each crop walks to the vault smiling.

**Two heavens:** the Commission's heaven = eternal life in the jar. The rebels' heaven = the real sky — nature, mortality, free will. Same word, opposite gods. "Heaven's Mandate" is the Commission's own name for the plan; Outer Heaven is life outside it.

**The ending:** the survivors learn the truth at the harvest and do the one thing off the curve — they die on their own terms, together (the novel's poisoned wine). The harvest fails. The Commission authors the next tragedy, and a kid retells the legend with a line nobody wrote: *"and the last stars went out on their own."*

## Proper-noun budget (nothing else gets invented)

**the Commission · Outer Heaven · Deviant · the 108 · the council** (the vault of brains — in dialogue usually just "the jars" / "the vault"). Person/place names (Timely Rain, Old Fang, the Southbank) don't count. BANNED words: stele, jianghu, Liangshan, HARMONY, Index, Render, Off-Network, NEO-LIANG.

## Voice rules

Plain words; one metaphor at a time; openers ≤80 words answering where/what/obstacle. Banter: ≤20 words/line, triple duty (moment + payoff + character), no lore in banter, write pairs, jokes beside darkness, never mock a loss. Names-stripped voice test must pass.

## The 42 chapters

Format: `# — New title — [debut/grant] — beat`. (Original TB name in parens for battle-setting flavor only.)

**Act 1 — Falling Off the Path (1–10)**
1. **Off the Path** (Borderlands) — [starters] — Legend told as bedtime story (2 lines). Nine Dragons' assigned future is revised mid-breakfast; removal squad; the Wanderer and the Skilled Doctor pull him off the grid. Pincer tutorial banter.
2. **Bail Money** (To the Capital) — [shberdan] — Red-Haired Devil, flagged for ferrying unlicensed medicine. First mention of Timely Rain — the man who posts bail for strangers.
3. **The Fraud Who Was Right** (Melting Pot) — [daiana] — Dragon in the Clouds flagged as a fraud because her readings keep coming true. The Commission does not license weather it didn't schedule.
4. **The Unicorn** (Into the Tower) — [bagunar; boss = Jade Unicorn, fight-then-join] — The perfect CEO framed by his own COO the day after a "risk consultancy" predicted his fall. He fights the crew in the tower lobby, cornered; beaten, he believes them.
5. **The Park at the Bottom** (Descent) — [macuri; NPCs Timely Rain + Tigress debut] — Down to the under-lake. The Unrestrained carries them through a flooded district that used to be his. Outer Heaven revealed: dead water park, warm kitchen. Seed: the ledger with 108 ruled lines.
6. **The Instructor** (The City Below) — [kem] — Panther Head: flagged for a murder he hasn't committed (94%), wife coveted by Young Gao, exile transfer through the undercity. Act 1 centerpiece. (Calibration sample.)
7. **Three Punches** (Molten Menace) — [gegonago joins — the Tattooed Monk, playable spear bruiser] — The Monk's episode: extortionist, three punches, stolen robes; he ambushes Panther Head's prison transfer at the furnace works and walks him out. Sworn-brother pair begins; he joins the roster.
8. **Repossession** (Labyrinth) — [zan] — Black Whirlwind refuses to repossess Timely Rain's bail office; brings the repossession order back "in pieces, for filing." One-step plans.
9. **The Tiger** (In Utero) — [korin] — The Pilgrim's viral bare-handed tiger takedown in the abandoned bio-farm — a beast the Commission's models said could not exist. Unregistered heroism = Deviance.
10. **Ten Feet of Steel** (Living Rock) — [samupi] — Scrapyard battle with a stone beast; she lassos Black Whirlwind out of danger mid-brawl and stays. Act 1 closes: Outer Heaven is a home now.

**Act 2 — The Brotherhood Works (11–24)**
11. **The Consultant** (To the Heart) — [eileen] — The Wisdom Star defects with two files: the birthday convoy's route, and a question ("Why did the Commission let me leave?"). Blue-Faced Beast introduced: disgraced captain auctioning his family sword; the crew fails to help him; the Commission rehires him for one job.
12. **Dungeons Deep** (Dungeons Deep) — [no grant] — Prison break: freeing a master the Commission disappeared. Banter golden age begins.
13. **The Birthday Convoy** (Kingslayer) — [no grant] — Heist part 1: the fake checkpoint, complimentary drinks. The Director's tribute convoy — seized relics, lineage swords — guarded by Blue-Faced Beast.
14. **Complimentary Drinks** (Downward Spiral) — [no grant] — Heist part 2: convoy taken without a death. Blue-Faced Beast wakes to ruin, flagged for "predicted complicity."
15. **The Fence Below** (The Maker) — [burbaba joins] — Fencing the relics in the deep market; returned swords relight old schools. Blue-Faced Beast tracks the crew — to join them. Seed: relics hum near certain people.
16. **Thunderbolt** (Animata) — [maralme; boss-then-join] — The Commission sends its champion; the crew beats him; the Commission burns his garrison for failing. He arrives at Outer Heaven with the ashes and his mace.
17. **The Marshal** (Intruder Alert!) — [nakupi; boss-then-join] — Twin Rods storms Outer Heaven; beaten honorably; sees enforcement burn a food line on his way out. Defects with both batons.
18. **The Range-Master** (The Oxsecians) — [harold] — Machine-corps crackdown. Little Li Guang refuses an arrest order on Timely Rain and proves where his aim lives with one impossible shot.
19. **The Voice Memo** (Separate Ways) — [no grant] — Timely Rain's leaked drunk memo ("if the path is written, who holds the pen?") earns a death sentence. The crew splits over rescue vs. hiding. Seed: the memo trended for exactly 94 seconds everywhere at once.
20. **The Runner** (Awakening) — [sorman] — Marvelous Traveler arrives with the execution date before it's announced. Race planning; invoice jokes against dread.
21. **The River Gate** (Strange New World) — [gigojago] — Approach by water. White Streak vs Black Whirlwind: the famous land-vs-water brawl, then friendship. 
22. **The Execution Ground** (Voice from Beyond) — [no grant] — Act 2 climax: forged court order broadcast mid-execution; Timely Rain pulled off the platform alive. The city cheers a rescue the Commission "somehow" never predicted. (In hindsight: it did.)
23. **Laws of the Jungle** (Laws of the Jungle) — [manmer] — Retreat through the wilds; Featherless Arrow covers it, stone by stone, and stays for the cause.
24. **After the Cheering** (Seek and Destroy) — [no grant] — Purge sweeps answer the rescue. Outer Heaven absorbs the refugees. Warm peak ends; the weather turns.

**Act 3 — The Squeeze (25–34)**
25. **Copies** (Zero Series) — [unasag] — The Commission fields synthetic replicas trained on the crew's own combat data. Living Death-God joins as the dock fleets are seized. "It knows our moves." First true dread.
26. **Flawless** (Evolution Prime) — [no grant] — The perfect replica duel — it fights like all of them at once. It loses to the one thing it wasn't fed: a joke at the wrong moment.
27. **The Kitchen Goes Dark** (Sorrow) — [no grant; Tigress dies] — A beast wave "no one predicted" hits the market while enforcement stands down. The Tigress dies shielding kids. First grief. Never mock it; the Monk carries the room.
28. **What Grief Does** (Vengeance) — [no grant] — The Pilgrim leads a retaliation that goes too far. The crew looks at itself. Dragon in the Clouds, quietly: "Our luck has a shape. Even the bad."
29. **The Last Good Cop** (Reboot) — [raprow] — Lord of the Beautiful Beard, burned by his precinct for old mercies, walks his beat straight into Outer Heaven.
30. **Folk Heroes** (Rising World) — [iskar] — The city starts leaving thank-you notes. Two-Headed Snake comes down from the hills with beast-sign maps: the monsters are being herded.
31. **Beast Country** (Beyond the Light) — [lan] — Mountain hunt; Twin-Tailed Scorpion saves the crew from a great beast driven mad by a Commission survey barge.
32. **The Southbank** (The Bridgehead) — [amazora] — First contact with the mirror brotherhood at the Witch's border inn — rougher, freer, unreadable. Old Fang's people. Two crews, one table, no fight. Yet.
33. **The Departed** (The Departed) — [no grant] — A Commission false-flag kills Southbank families; each side blames the other; the Witch's inn burns. The Wisdom Star: "Someone is writing us into a war."
34. **The Offer** (The Light of Devotion) — [zenzoze] — Gold Lancer arrives to teach anti-armor technique — followed by the Director's envoy: amnesty, legal identities, enforcer badges. Price: the Southbank. Act ends on the vote's eve.

**Act 4 — The Amnesty and the Harvest (35–42)**
35. **The Vote** (Malevolence Alights) — [no grant] — Timely Rain argues FOR, over Black Whirlwind's fury. The badges arrive. First joint op under Commission oversight feels like wearing someone else's skin.
36. **The War We Wanted** (Permeating Fear) — [Fiery Thunderbolt falls] — Southbank campaign, phase 1. Victories on schedule. Fiery Thunderbolt dies buying a bridge; the Commission logs it as "within parameters."
37. **The Water Gate** (In Devil's Grip) — [White Streak + Red-Haired Devil fall] — Campaign climax. Old Fang falls; his last words: "They promised you heaven too?" White Streak dies holding the water gate — the river takes him home. Banter thins.
38. **Counting** (Animata Tears) — [no grant] — The dead are named on the park wall. The Wisdom Star cracks it: every victory on a curve, a roster of 108, the ledger matching line for line. The ascension invitation arrives, gilt-edged.
39. **Ascension Day** (Proposition) — [no grant] — The ceremony. The vault opens: jars, centuries of minds, the founder's own recording of the star legend, Timely Rain's kind life read back as a script. Everything was the plan. Fight out or kneel.
40. **The Harvest** (Ash of Souls) — [Twin Rods, Living Death-God, Gold Lancer fall] — Council guardians and harvest machinery; the crew carries the truth out of the tower through everything the Commission owns.
41. **The Last Cup** (A Tower Reborn) — [no grant] — The refusal. Survivors break the harvest engine, then share one cup on the park's dry wave pool — the one act off the curve. Timely Rain drinks first. Black Whirlwind refuses to be left behind. Callback: "Stand on my other side. One last time."
42. **Outer Heaven** (Terra Battle) — [no grant] — Final battle framed as the story the city tells afterward — the last stand nobody filmed. Epilogue: new district, new tragedy, a kid retells the legend: *"and the last stars went out on their own."* Something very old checks its math again.
